Shaders & Image Effects//

This page is dedicated entirely to the users! Writing shaders has always been one of my passions and has become one of the most useful skills I have. Shaders and image effects are used to make things look pretty. They can add stylized art to our games, or even optimize other parts of our projects. I make different shaders to teach people about the amazing things they can do and how to implement them in their own projects.

Sin City Effect

Image effect · Unity · CG

An image effect that creates a scene like Frank Miller's comic-inspired movie “Sin City”. Easy to implement, written for people who already have a basic understanding of coding image effects.
