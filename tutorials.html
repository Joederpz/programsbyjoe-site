Shaders and Image Effects

This is a page dedicated entirely to the users! Writing shaders has always been one of my passions and it has become one of the most useful skills I have. Shaders and image effects quite simply are used to make things look pretty. They can add stylized art to our games or they can even be used to optimize other parts of our projects. I will be making different shaders to teach people about the amazing things that can be done with them and how we can implement them into our own projects. 

Sin City Effect

This is an image effect that will create a scene similar to Frank Miller's comic inspired movie "Sin City". Overall it is very easy to implement but I have written this tutorial for users who already have a basic understanding of coding image effects. Click here to see how to code it yourself!
